# SEED-AI-06 · The change request says whether, and how far, an assistant was used

## Do not

Leave the reviewer to guess.

## Do instead

The change request states whether there was assistance and its scope. It is context for the
reviewer, not a judgement.

## Why

It tells the reviewer where to look harder.

## How it is checked

The change request template.
