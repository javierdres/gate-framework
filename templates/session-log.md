# <Topic> (<DD/MM/YYYY>)

The person's log: one file per topic and start date, in `personal/sessions/`. The assistant
writes it without being asked whenever the topic moves forward, and reads it before resuming.
It is local and ignored by version control. Link to tickets and change requests; do not copy
them.

## Request
What was wanted, in one or two sentences.

## Decisions
- What was decided and why. What was discarded, with its reason.

## Status
Where each thing stands: branches, change requests, tickets, files, deliverables.

## Critical
- What cannot wait. Omitted if there is nothing.

## Pending
- What is missing and who it depends on.
