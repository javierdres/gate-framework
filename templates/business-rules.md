# Business rules · <Product>

How the product works, as the product owner describes it. Each rule is validated or pending
validation, and is updated the same week it changes.

Questions to start with, for the product owner:

- Which processes does the product cover, and what are their states from start to end?
- Who can do what, depending on their profile?
- What happens when a step fails or a third party does not answer?
- Which steps are still manual, and why?
